### 📆 Leap Year Logic (Gregorian Calendar)
- Learned the rules to identify leap years:
  - Year divisible by 4 is a leap year  
  - Except when divisible by 100 (not a leap year)  
  - Unless also divisible by 400 (then it is a leap year)  

- Practiced creating a function to check leap year conditions  

- Understood examples such as:
  - Leap years: 2000, 2400  
  - Not leap years: 1900, 2100, 2200, 2300, 2500
